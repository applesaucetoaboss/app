Privacy Policy

This policy explains how the app handles data.

Overview

The app provides internet failover diagnostics and a simple proxy fetch feature. We do not sell or share personal data. Minimal, non-personal operational data may be used to deliver app functionality.

Data We Handle

  • Connection metrics (e.g., latency, availability) to recommend a route.
  • Basic request metadata for proxy fetch (status code and headers only).
  • No storage of page content fetched via proxy beyond transient processing.

Advertising

The mobile app uses rewarded ads via Google AdMob to unlock certain actions. AdMob and its partners may collect device identifiers and usage data to deliver and measure ads. See Google’s policies: Ads & Cookies and AdMob Policy.

Security

We use reasonable technical measures and avoid storing unnecessary personal data. DNS over HTTPS and proxy testing are performed via public endpoints for connectivity checks.

Your Choices

  • You can use the app without signing in.
  • You may decline rewarded ads; core diagnostics remain accessible.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact the developer via the store listing’s email.
